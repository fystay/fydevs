# FY Devs — Core Project Brief

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Initial foundation  
**Project:** FY Devs (F-Y-D-E-V-S)  
**Primary market assumption:** UK  
**Currency assumption:** GBP (£), subject to confirmation

---

## 1. Project Overview

FY Devs is a digital business with two primary revenue streams:

1. **Bespoke website design and development** for businesses seeking distinctive, premium-quality websites.
2. **Booking software and SaaS products**, initially including a driver and operator application developed for an airport taxi business, with potential applications across taxi firms and other booking-based businesses.

FY Devs is not intended to compete in the cheap, template-based website market. Its focus is on bespoke digital experiences, thoughtful design, useful functionality, and technology that solves real business problems.

The website should act as a premium showroom and sales platform. Visitors should be able to explore projects, understand services, configure their requirements, see estimated pricing, and purchase or enquire without unnecessary friction.

## 2. Vision and Positioning

### Proposed positioning

Bespoke digital experiences and practical software, built around the way your business works.

FY Devs combines premium web design with custom development and booking software.

The brand should communicate quality, creativity, technical competence, and a genuine commitment to understanding each client's needs.

### Core principles

- Bespoke over generic: avoid presenting projects as reskinned templates.
- Clarity over friction: make services, prices, and next steps easy to understand.
- Business outcomes: prioritise credibility, enquiries, sales, customer experience, and operational efficiency.
- Premium but approachable: high-quality work without unnecessarily complicated sales processes.
- Purposeful technology: build features that solve real problems.
- Transparent pricing: explain costs, assumptions, exclusions, and ongoing commitments.
- Practical delivery: keep the initial product focused and expand when justified.

## 3. Target Audiences

### Website development

Primary audience: businesses and potential clients seeking professional websites or tailored digital solutions.

Typical needs include:

- A modern, credible online presence.
- A distinctive website that reflects their brand.
- More enquiries, bookings, or sales.
- Clear package options and immediate indicative pricing.
- Hosting, maintenance, and ongoing support.
- Custom functionality, integrations, or booking systems.

### Booking software and SaaS

Businesses that manage bookings and operations, initially focusing on airport taxi and taxi businesses.

Potential future audiences include other booking-led businesses, provided the software genuinely supports their workflows.

The website must distinguish between purchasing the existing SaaS product and commissioning a bespoke booking system.

## 4. Website Objectives

1. Demonstrate the quality of FY Devs' work immediately.
2. Showcase selected projects through strong visuals and concise case studies.
3. Explain services quickly and clearly.
4. Offer an interactive website configurator with instant indicative pricing.
5. Allow direct purchases for clearly defined packages.
6. Provide a scope-confirmation process for complex bespoke projects.
7. Give booking-software visitors a dedicated product journey.
8. Explain hosting, maintenance, payment terms, and ongoing costs.
9. Deliver an excellent mobile experience.
10. Maintain accessibility, performance, security, and search-engine foundations.

## 5. Proposed Website Structure

### Home
Introduce FY Devs, communicate the value proposition, showcase selected work, and direct visitors towards website development or booking software.

### Websites
Explain bespoke website services, the development process, package options, and pricing configurator.

### Projects / Our Work
Showcase completed projects with high-quality imagery, the client's problem, the solution, and verified outcomes where available.

### Build Your Website
Provide the interactive questionnaire, live estimate, package recommendations, and purchase or enquiry process.

### Booking Software
Present the driver/operator and booking application, its intended audience, verified capabilities, pricing, and next steps.

### Bespoke Systems
Explain custom booking platforms, integrations, and tailored software development.

### About FY Devs
Communicate the motivation behind the business and its commitment to creating websites clients are proud of.

### Contact / Project Summary
Provide a straightforward way to ask questions or submit a project brief.

### Legal
Include appropriate privacy information, terms of service, payment terms, cancellation/refund policies, and cookie information where applicable.

### Navigation priorities

Make the two principal customer journeys immediately obvious:

- Build a website
- Booking software

Booking-software visitors should not need to navigate through website packages to find the product.

## 6. Visual Identity and UX Direction

### Design inspiration

The desired aesthetic is modern technology, informed by qualities associated with Apple and Anthropic.

Use these as inspiration rather than copying their branding or interfaces.

### Design principles

- Premium, restrained visual identity.
- Strong typography and clear information hierarchy.
- Generous spacing and considered layouts.
- High-quality project imagery and product screenshots.
- Concise, confident copy.
- Subtle, purposeful animation.
- Accessible contrast and visible keyboard focus.
- Responsive layouts designed for mobile and desktop.
- Clear buttons, interactive states, and feedback.
- Fast loading and minimal unnecessary visual clutter.

The final colour palette, typography, and component system remain to be decided.

Avoid generic agency clichés, excessive animation, vague claims, and unnecessary complexity.

## 7. Website Packages and Pricing

**Status: proposed structure. Final prices and deliverables must be confirmed before publication.**

Start with three packages that provide clear entry points without restricting bespoke work.

### Essential

For businesses needing a polished, professional web presence.

Potential inclusions:

- A defined number of pages.
- Bespoke visual direction.
- Responsive implementation.
- Contact or enquiry forms.
- Basic technical SEO foundations.
- Agreed launch and handover support.

### Professional

For businesses needing a more developed marketing website.

Potential additions:

- Additional pages and custom layouts.
- Richer service and project presentation.
- Enhanced lead-generation features.
- Content management where appropriate.
- Additional analytics or integrations.

### Premium

For businesses requiring a more distinctive digital experience or advanced functionality.

Potential additions:

- Extensive custom design and interaction.
- More complex page structures.
- Advanced integrations.
- Bespoke functionality.
- Enhanced testing and launch support.

Package descriptions must define deliverables and limits clearly.

Do not promise unlimited pages, revisions, content changes, or undefined functionality.

### Recommended pricing strategy

Use a hybrid model:

- Display package prices or starting prices once commercially validated.
- Provide an interactive estimator for projects with variable scope.
- Calculate an indicative price immediately as customers configure their requirements.
- Explain which choices affect the price.
- Clearly distinguish one-off build costs from recurring charges.
- Show inclusions, exclusions, assumptions, and optional extras.
- Allow customers to review and edit their answers.
- Support direct checkout for defined packages.
- Require scope confirmation for complex bespoke projects before a binding final price or payment.

An estimate must not be presented as a guaranteed final quote unless the project scope and pricing rules support that commitment.

## 8. Interactive Website Configurator

The configurator is a key part of the FY Devs customer experience.

Its purpose is to make purchasing straightforward and remove the uncertainty associated with requesting a traditional agency quote.

### Recommended customer journey

**Step 1: Choose the project type**

Options may include:

- Business website
- Portfolio website
- Online shop
- Booking website
- Bespoke web application
- Unsure / need guidance

**Step 2: Describe the business**

Ask what the business does and what the website should achieve.

**Step 3: Select size and content requirements**

Ask about approximate page count, existing content, copywriting, and imagery.

**Step 4: Choose functionality**

Potential options include:

- Enquiry forms
- Content management
- Online payments
- Customer accounts
- Booking functionality
- E-commerce
- Third-party integrations
- Custom features

Only show relevant follow-up questions.

**Step 5: Define the design requirements**

Ask about existing branding, visual references, and desired levels of custom design and interaction.

**Step 6: Select hosting and ongoing care**

Offer optional or bundled hosting and maintenance plans.

**Step 7: Establish timing**

Ask about the desired launch date without guaranteeing unverified delivery dates.

**Step 8: Show the estimated price**

Display:

- Estimated build price.
- Selected package.
- Selected extras.
- Ongoing costs.
- What's included.
- Important assumptions and exclusions.
- Expected next steps.

**Step 9: Purchase or submit the project**

For defined packages, allow the customer to proceed to checkout.

For complex bespoke work, provide a complete project summary and request scope confirmation before finalising the price.

### Configurator UX requirements

- Keep questions concise and understandable.
- Use progressive disclosure to avoid overwhelming users.
- Show progress through the questionnaire.
- Update pricing as relevant answers change.
- Allow previous answers to be edited.
- Explain optional extras clearly.
- Preserve the completed project summary.
- Validate all calculations before checkout.
- Provide helpful errors and recovery options.
- Make the experience accessible and mobile-friendly.

## 9. Hosting and Ongoing Maintenance

**Status: proposed commercial model. Prices and service levels remain undecided.**

Separate one-off development fees from recurring hosting and maintenance charges.

Potential plans may cover:

- Website hosting.
- SSL configuration.
- Backups.
- Uptime monitoring.
- Routine updates where applicable.
- A defined allowance for small content changes.
- Support with clearly stated response expectations.

Optional higher-tier services could include enhanced maintenance, additional support, or ongoing development.

Clearly disclose whether the following are included or charged separately:

- Domain registration.
- Business email.
- Paid plugins or software licences.
- Third-party subscriptions.
- Usage-based service charges.
- Major new features.
- Work outside the agreed maintenance allowance.

Pricing should reflect actual infrastructure costs, support commitments, maintenance workload, risk, and business margin.

Confirm VAT treatment before publishing prices.

## 10. Booking Software and SaaS

The booking software requires its own dedicated landing page and sales journey.

The initial product includes a driver and operator application developed for an airport taxi business.

Potential applications include other taxi firms and booking-led businesses, subject to validating the product's capabilities and fit.

### Product page requirements

Explain, once confirmed:

- The businesses the software serves.
- The problems it solves.
- Driver workflows.
- Operator workflows.
- Booking management and dispatch capabilities.
- Customer booking functionality, if supported.
- Supported devices and integrations.
- Pricing and subscription terms.
- Onboarding and support.
- Demo, trial, or purchase options.

Do not advertise features that have not been implemented or verified.

### SaaS versus bespoke systems

**SaaS product**

A repeatable software product with a defined feature set and a validated pricing model.

**Bespoke booking system**

A tailored solution built for an individual business's requirements, integrations, and operational workflows.

Visitors looking for taxi dispatch, airport transfers, or driver/operator software should be directed towards the SaaS product.

Visitors with unique operational requirements should be offered a bespoke systems enquiry.

## 11. Purchase and Payment Flow

The objective is to allow customers to understand their requirements, see the cost, and purchase without unnecessary back-and-forth.

### Fixed-scope packages

Where deliverables, pricing, availability, and terms are clear, customers should be able to purchase directly.

### Bespoke projects

For complex projects, confirm the scope, assumptions, exclusions, and final price before asking the customer to make a binding commitment.

### Deposits and payment terms

If deposits are offered, clearly explain:

- Deposit amount.
- Payment schedule.
- When work begins.
- Balance payment terms.
- Cancellation and refund conditions.

### Order confirmation

Customers should receive:

- Confirmation of their order or enquiry.
- Their selected package and extras.
- A summary of their project requirements.
- Relevant pricing and payment terms.
- Clear next steps.

### Backend requirements

- Route enquiries and orders to a reliable business inbox or suitable workflow.
- Validate pricing and order details on the server.
- Use a reputable payment provider.
- Verify payment notifications before marking orders as paid.
- Never store raw payment card details.
- Collect only the personal data needed for the transaction.
- Provide clear failure and retry states.

Do not accept payment for materially ambiguous work without an appropriate scope-confirmation step.

## 13. Technical Principles

The final technology stack has not yet been confirmed.

Choose technology based on the existing project, maintainability, performance, security, deployment requirements, and integrations.

### Architecture and implementation

- Keep the marketing website fast and accessible.
- Maintain pricing rules in one authoritative, testable source.
- Do not duplicate pricing calculations across components.
- Treat browser-side estimates as informational.
- Recalculate and validate order totals on the server.
- Validate all user inputs.
- Protect enquiry forms against spam and abuse.
- Keep credentials in environment variables or a suitable secret manager.
- Never commit secrets to source control.
- Apply least-privilege access.
- Implement suitable logging, backups, and data retention.
- Separate the marketing website from the operational SaaS application and its data unless integration is justified.
- Test pricing calculations, checkout failures, mobile layouts, keyboard navigation, and error states.

### Payment security

Use a reputable payment provider and its supported checkout and webhook mechanisms.

Do not treat a browser redirect or client-side success message as proof that payment has succeeded.
